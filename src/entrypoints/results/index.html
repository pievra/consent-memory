Sending your results to the Pievra team

  1. Press Download with site names. The team needs the names to know which site each result is for.
  2. A file called pievra-results-…json is saved to your downloads.
  3. Email that file to contact@pievra.com. It is not the same file as “Export my choices” in the settings.

With site names, the file shows which of the sites below you visited, and your browser’s language and time zone. Without them, sites are numbered instead. Nothing is sent by this page: the file is only saved on your device, and sending it is your choice. What we do with a file you send is in the privacy policy.

What the results mean

Refused and confirmed
Refusal sent, and the banner product confirmed it stored the refusal.
Partly done
Refusal stored, but something remains (for example “legitimate interest” still declared), or you allowed a purpose that could not be granted separately.
Applied, not confirmed
The refusal steps ran, usually inside a banner frame that then closed, and nothing could confirm what was stored. Not counted as a success.
Not accepted
You allow everything on the site, and Pievra has no way to press “accept” on this banner product. Nothing was clicked.
Accept or pay
The site offers no free refusal. Nothing was clicked.
Could not manage consent
The action did not complete, or the banner product reports the refusal was not stored.
Left alone
Left alone on purpose, for example a password field was filled in.
No banner open
A banner product is present but no banner was open.
